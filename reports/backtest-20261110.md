# Backtest 2026-11-10

## What was asked

The first three-symbol run (BTC, ETH, SOL, about 110 days, $800) lost money. Most exits were the 4H trail firing before the 2R partial, and Setup B (trend pullback) was the worst sleeve. This study replays the same rules on the Vision USDT-M archive, then compares a small set of looser exits and four pullback filters. Nothing here places an order.

## Data

Source is `data.binance.vision` monthly USD-M klines and funding, 2019-09 through 2026-10. The archive listing contains 923 USDT-M perpetual names, including contracts that later stopped printing bars. Daily files were stored for the names Vision still hosts. A name was downloaded on 1h, 4h, and funding only when its best UTC day had at least $500,000 of quote volume, which is half the $1M Setup D gate: a 24h window sits inside two UTC days, so a name below that ceiling can never pass the gate. 920 names cleared it. 920 of those had 1h, 4h, and 1d bars and were scanned. 31 of the scanned names are inferred delisted (last daily bar more than three days before the archive's latest daily bar). `exchangeInfo` was not used: this environment receives HTTP 451 from `fapi.binance.com`, and the archive does not include it.

Sample span 2020-01-01T00:00:00+00:00 to 2026-10-31T23:59:59.999000+00:00 (2496.0 days, 25 walk-forward folds of 182 in-sample and 91 out-of-sample days). Signals emitted after the static gates: 208447. Setup D chart hits: 111103. Every one of those stays rejected because on-chain checks are missing.

## Costs and path rules

Every fill pays the 0.05% taker fee per side, half of the 0.05% assumed spread, and 0.01% slippage. Funding uses the settled rate both as the last settled rate and as the predicted rate (`funding_predicted_model: persist_last_settled`), because the predicted rate is not in the archive. A stop and a target in the same bar fill the stop. A stop that gaps through fills at the open. The trail updates on a closed 4H bar and can only fill on a later bar. The hard stop never moves to a worse price. A partial, once filled, moves the stop to breakeven when that tightens it. News vetoes are not modeled (`backtest.news_status: ALLOW` is an assumption). Open trades at the last bar are marked `end_of_data`.

## Selection rule

This rule was written into `desk.backtest.selection` before the grid was scored. A fold votes only when a variant has enough in-sample trades (15 for an exit, 10 for a Setup B variant). The vote goes to the highest in-sample expectancy. A tie stays on the baseline. A variant is adopted only if it wins a strict majority of voting folds and its frozen pooled out-of-sample record has at least 30 trades, positive expectancy, expectancy above the baseline, and max drawdown no more than 5 points worse than the baseline. Out-of-sample numbers are not used to pick the winner. Pooled out-of-sample trades are those entered in the non-overlapping 91-day windows. In-sample windows overlap, so the per-fold vote can see a trade in more than one in-sample window; the pooled in-sample row in the CSV counts each trade once and is descriptive only.

Slice metrics restart at $800 and include only that slice's cashflows, with flat days filled in. A slice can show a drawdown above 100% when its dollar losses exceed $800. That happens if those trades were sized on a portfolio that was larger than $800 at the time. It is not the portfolio's own drawdown. The portfolio drawdown is the full-sample row for `ema_immediate_2r`.

Dollar PnL and Sharpe are path-dependent. A sleeve can make dollars and still have a negative average R when the winners happen at a larger account and the losers happen later, on a smaller one. The adoption rule uses expectancy in R, not dollars.

## Headline

Baseline `ema_immediate_2r` on the full sample: 10,512 trades, win rate 35.3%, expectancy **-0.076R**, max drawdown **100%**, Sharpe -3.20, net PnL **-$800** on an $800 account. Pooled out of sample: 10,316 trades, expectancy **-0.076R**, max drawdown 100%, net PnL -$800.

The account goes to about zero because a small negative R, risked at 2% of a shrinking equity, compounds. `0.9985^10000` is already dust. Every exit variant in the grid has a negative out-of-sample expectancy and ends in the same place. Looser trails cut the trade count (positions stay open longer, so fewer new entries) and do not turn the expectancy positive. The least-negative out-of-sample expectancy is `chandelier_3_after_1r_1r` at **-0.051R**. That is still a loss, and it did not win a majority of the in-sample folds (5 of 24 votes).

## Exit grid

Chandelier trails ratchet off the best 4H high or low since entry, at 2x or 3x ATR, and are armed only after 1R so they are not a second hard stop. ### Why the baseline pullbacks look the way they do

On the full baseline sample there are 3,485 pullback trades.

The 4H trail is still the exit. 2,869 trades leave by `trail_stop`, 432 by the original hard stop, 169 by invalidation, 11 by the time stop, and 4 are still open at the last bar. Only 590 partials fill (17%). Of the trail exits, 2,281 happen before the partial (65%). Another 600 trades reach a favorable excursion of 1R and still never fill the partial. Mean MFE is 1.41R and mean MAE is 0.52R, so the typical trade does travel. The stop sits about 1.0 ATR from the fill, which is inside the 3 ATR cap and tight enough that the EMA trail can catch it before 2R.

Longs: 1,663 trades, win rate 20.9%, expectancy -0.069R. Shorts: 1,822 trades, win rate 22.2%, expectancy -0.037R.

BTC regime, same trades: long-favored -0.067R (1,381), short-favored -0.080R (1,366), caution **+0.025R** (738). Caution is the least-bad bucket. B2, which drops caution, is worse out of sample (-0.068R) than the baseline (-0.053R). That split was not used to invent a "trade only caution" variant.

Fast-EMA tags: 3,126 trades, -0.052R. Mid-EMA only: 359 trades, -0.058R. Mean fast-EMA gap is 0.72 ATR on winners and 0.76 ATR on losers. The losers are not the flat trends. B1 (gap at least 0.5 ATR) won the most in-sample folds (9 of 24, not a majority) and its out-of-sample expectancy is -0.053R, the same loss as the baseline, on fewer trades.

B3's wider initial stop does not help: out-of-sample expectancy -0.046R, still below zero, and it was not the majority in-sample winner. B4 is worse (-0.069R). None of the four filters is an edge.

## What is evidence, and what is not

No variant cleared the out-of-sample rule, and every exit variant's out-of-sample expectancy is negative. That is evidence against these rules as a traded system on this archive and this cost model. It is not evidence that some other exit, found by reading this table and picking the least-bad row, would work. `chandelier_3_after_1r_1r` is the least-bad expectancy here and it still loses.

The pullback sleeve's positive dollar PnL and positive Sharpe are not evidence of an edge. Expectancy in R is negative for every Setup B variant, in the vote and out of sample. A positive dollar path with a negative average R means the winners were taken while the account was larger.

A higher in-sample expectancy, a single fold, or the best out-of-sample row is not a reason to change a default. Twelve exit variants and five pullback variants were scored. That is small next to a continuous parameter search, and it is still large enough that one of them can win a few folds by chance. The majority vote plus the frozen out-of-sample check is what keeps that from becoming the default. B1's 9 votes and the chandelier's 5 votes are pluralities, not majorities.

## Recommended settings

Leave `config/settings.yaml` on the v0.3 exit and the v0.3 pullback. Exit stays `trail_mode: ema`, `trail_arm: immediate`, `partial_at_r: 2.0`. Setup B stays min_ema_gap_atr 0, allow_caution true, stop_swing_bars 1, require_fast_ema_tag false. The exit winner and the Setup B winner were not resimulated as one combined configuration on a fresh out-of-sample slice. Do not treat the pair as a jointly tested system.

## Caveats

- Delisted versus still listed is inferred from the last Vision daily bar, not from exchangeInfo. A contract that died in the last three days of the archive can be labeled TRADING.
- Names whose best day was under the download floor are absent. The per-bar volume gate still rejects thin days of the names that were downloaded.
- The spread is the configured 0.05% assumption on every fill. Historical books are not in the archive. Quiet names are charged too little; violent names are charged too little as well.
- Predicted funding is the last settled rate, held until the next settlement. That is disclosed in the cost block and it is not the exchange's predicted rate.
- News vetoes and Setup D on-chain checks are not historical series. Setup D contributes no trades.
- Same-bar stop-before-target fills the stop when both prices print. The path inside the bar is not known.
- A trail computed from a 4H close is not active during that same bar. A wick through the new stop on the bar that creates it is not a fill.
- The research clock walks the union of hourly opens and skips hours where the account is flat and no signal is due. Flat days are filled back in before Sharpe and drawdown.
- Symbols are considered in alphabetical order when several signal on the same hour. That tie-break can change which name gets the last slot under the risk cap.
- In-sample windows overlap by design of the 182/91 split. The vote uses each fold's own window. Do not add the fold expectancies into one pooled in-sample trade count.
- This process refuses mode live and has no order path, no API key, and no withdrawal.
- A passing test and a positive in-sample expectancy are not an edge.
- One RSI calculation overflowed during the scan (`avg_gain / avg_loss` on a near-zero loss). The value that falls out of that case is RSI 100, which cannot pass the pullback RSI band. The scan was not rerun after the guard was added.
- The archive's 923 names are what Vision still hosts. Only 31 stop more than three days before 2026-10-31. Names such as LUNAUSDT, SRMUSDT, HNTUSDT, YFIIUSDT, and AKROUSDT are in that 31. Names such as FTTUSDT and RAYUSDT still have files through 2026-10-31 in this bucket. Contracts Vision has deleted are not in the study.
- BTC daily files in this bucket start on 2020-01-01, not 2019-09. The fold clock starts there.
